# Apex — Finance

Module spec (LIFE-7). Status: **signed off 2026-08-02; E2 built** (see the build plan). The schema draft below was the contract for LIFE-18 and the E2 build tickets; the applied migrations in `supabase/migrations/` are now authoritative.

## Purpose

The household's money at a glance: what's in the accounts, what's leaving and when, how savings and the mortgage are progressing. Built ADHD-first — the app must *reduce* the effort of staying on top of money, never add to it.

## Design philosophy — the card contract

Apex defines the design language for every future module body. Every surface is a stack/grid of **isolated cards**, each obeying this contract:

1. **One card answers one question.** "How much is left of the mortgage?" "What's due this week?" A card that answers two questions is two cards.
2. **Self-contained.** A card reads from shared data (`lib/apex/*` helpers) but holds no dependency on sibling cards — it can be added, removed, or reordered without touching anything else. One server component per card.
3. **Glanceable.** The answer is the biggest thing on the card: a number, a bar, a date. Supporting text is one line, 13px, muted. No paragraphs, ever.
4. **At most one quick action**, performed *in place* (popover/drawer) — never a redirect. A distracted user must be able to finish the thought where they had it.
5. **Bounded.** Cards have small fixed heights; a page reads top-to-bottom in seconds. Detail lives behind the card (drawer), not on it.
6. **Creation happens in a modal or drawer**, with the minimum viable fields — everything optional that can be optional. Filling in data must feel like two clicks, not a form.
7. Design bar rules apply: density per [conventions](../conventions.md#design-bar), Apex emerald accent from the registry, house spring motion, light + dark.

## Decisions log

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Hybrid balances** — accounts store a `balance`; logged transactions move it (DB trigger); a two-click "Sync balance" corrects it anytime via an `adjustment` transaction | A pure ledger punishes missed weeks — exactly the ADHD failure mode. Re-syncing is guilt-free and keeps history consistent |
| 2 | **Transactions anchor to accounts; card is an optional tag** | Direct debits, standing orders and transfers have no card; account totals must always add up. Card filtering still works when tagged |
| 3 | **Mortgages support shared ownership and standard repayment in one shape** — `equity_share_pct`/`rent_monthly` nullable; null share = 100% owned. Staircasing = updating those fields (event noted in `metadata`) | John's answer: UK-typical shared ownership that can staircase out into a standard repayment structure. One row's lifecycle covers both |
| 4 | **Recurring = schedule + mark-paid** — due items surface with one-tap "Mark paid" that creates the transaction and rolls `next_due_on`; nothing auto-posts | Nothing happens silently; the due list doubles as a monthly checklist |
| 5 | **GBP only for MVP** — `currency` lives on `accounts` (default `'GBP'`) per data-standards; all other amounts inherit. Multi-currency is a later promotion | Household is UK-based; per-amount currency columns today are speculative |
| 6 | Transaction kinds: `income · expense · transfer · adjustment` — `adjustment` is system-generated by Sync balance only, excluded from spend analytics | Keeps John's three user-facing kinds clean while making hybrid balances auditable |
| 7 | Cards store **`last4` only, never a full card number** (plus brand/expiry for the visual + expiry alerts) | Security; a PAN has no business being in this database |
| 8 | Cancelling a subscription/bill = **soft delete** (history transactions remain); no `is_active` flag | Follows the soft-delete standard; avoids a bespoke flag |
| 9 | Default categories are seeded per space, then fully editable. *(Amended 2026-08-04: the lazy on-first-visit call never happened in practice, so seeding moved to a DB trigger on space creation — migration `20260804220000`.)* | Sensible starting point without setup effort |
| 10 | Mortgage trajectory / overpayment what-ifs are **client-side amortization math** — nothing stored | Projections are derived, not data |
| 11 | Sign-off (2026-08-02): default category list approved as proposed; **account colors reuse the six space swatches**; loans stay bill-level for MVP (no first-class loan entity) | John's answers to the open questions |

## Pages

Sidebar nav (registry-driven from `lib/modules.ts`):
**Overview · Transactions · Accounts & Cards · Subscriptions & Bills · Budgets & Savings · Mortgage**

### Overview (dashboard)

Read top-to-bottom in under ten seconds:

- **Total balance** — sum across accounts, with per-account mini-breakdown
- **Due soon** — next 7 days of subscriptions/bills, each row with one-tap Mark paid
- **This month** — spend vs. budget, one bar per budgeted category (top 4–5)
- **Savings strip** — one compact progress tile per goal
- **Mortgage snapshot** — balance remaining + months until the rate ends

### Transactions

Filterable table (account, card, category, kind, month) + **Add transaction** drawer: amount, kind, account, description — date defaults to today, category optional, card optional. Transfers pick a destination account.

### Accounts & Cards

The showcase page — visual, not analytical:

- **Account cards** — one per account: name, institution, kind, balance, account color. Quick actions: Sync balance (popover: "what's the real balance?" → creates adjustment), Transfer (drawer: from/to/amount).
- **Bank-card visuals** — rendered card art per physical/virtual card: brand, last4, expiry, color, linked account. Expiring-soon state when < 60 days out.
- Create account / add card in a drawer.

### Subscriptions & Bills

- **Header cards:** monthly subscriptions total · monthly bills total · next payment due
- Two lists in one table, split by kind — **subscription** (optional: Netflix, Disney+) vs **bill** (committed: mortgage, electricity, council tax, car). Each row: name, amount, cadence, next due, paying account. Due/overdue rows get the Mark paid action inline.

### Budgets & Savings

- **Budgets (top):** per-category monthly envelopes — bar per category: spent vs. `amount`, current month. Over-budget state is visible but calm, not shaming.
- **Saving goals (bottom):** one card per goal — the **progress grid** (target divided into cells; cells fill as savings grow), amount saved / target, optional target date with an on-track hint. Quick action: **Top up** (drawer: from-account + amount → creates a transfer to the linked account, or bumps `saved_amount` when unlinked). No redirects.

### Mortgage

One stack of isolated cards per mortgage. This list was the pre-build sketch;
the shipped page grew past it under [mortgage.md](apex/mortgage.md)'s research
(headline payment-shock card, payment split, cost-ahead chart, loan to value
staircase, equity, rent, the what-if with a finish-flag ruler, monthly cost,
and the paperwork record). That doc's status block is the current truth; the
page itself is the approved design reference for every future module surface.

## Entities & schema draft

All tables use the [standard base shape](../data-standards.md#standard-table-shape) (`id`, `space_id`, `notes`, `metadata`, `created_by`, soft delete, timestamps, `set_updated_at`) and membership-scoped RLS. Domain columns only below.

> The draft below is the signed-off contract, kept for the rationale; the migrations are authoritative and diverge deliberately. Child-table FKs are composite pairs — `(account_id, space_id)`, `(category_id, space_id)`, `(card_id, account_id)` — against `unique (id, space_id)` on the parent, so a row can never reference another space's data. Columns added since: `transactions.recurring_payment_id` (LIFE-27), `recurring_payments.anchor_day` (migration `20260804210000`), and the mortgage headline fields `repayment_type`, `balance_as_of`, `reversion_rate`, `rate_started_on` (migration `20260805090000`).

```sql
-- accounts: one row per real-world account
name         text not null,
kind         text not null check (kind in ('current','savings','credit_card','investment','cash')),
institution  text,                          -- "Monzo", "Barclays"; null for cash
balance      bigint not null default 0,     -- pence, signed; credit cards go negative when owed
currency     char(3) not null default 'GBP',
color        text not null                  -- hex, like spaces.color; drives the account card visual
```

```sql
-- cards: physical/virtual cards under an account
account_id   uuid not null references accounts (id) on delete cascade,
name         text not null,                 -- "John's Monzo"
brand        text check (brand in ('visa','mastercard','amex','other')),
last4        char(4),                       -- display only; full numbers are never stored
expires_on   date,                          -- month precision; powers expiring-soon alerts
color        text not null
```

```sql
-- categories: per-space spend/income taxonomy (lazily seeded, editable)
name         text not null,
kind         text not null check (kind in ('income','expense')),
color        text not null,
icon         text not null                  -- lucide icon name
```

```sql
-- transactions: the ledger; every row moves its account's balance via trigger
account_id            uuid not null references accounts (id) on delete cascade,
card_id               uuid references cards (id),           -- optional tag: which card was used
kind                  text not null check (kind in ('income','expense','transfer','adjustment')),
amount                bigint not null,                      -- pence; >0, direction from kind — except adjustments, which carry the signed sync delta
description           text not null,                        -- payee/what: "Tesco", "Salary"
category_id           uuid references categories (id),
occurred_on           date not null default current_date,
transfer_account_id   uuid references accounts (id),        -- destination; required iff kind='transfer'
recurring_payment_id  uuid references recurring_payments (id) -- set by Mark paid; powers payment history (column added by LIFE-27's migration)
```

```sql
-- recurring_payments: subscriptions & bills schedule (cancel = soft delete)
name         text not null,
kind         text not null check (kind in ('subscription','bill')),
amount       bigint not null,
cadence      text not null check (cadence in ('weekly','monthly','quarterly','yearly')),
next_due_on  date not null,
account_id   uuid references accounts (id),  -- which account pays
category_id  uuid references categories (id) -- applied to the transaction on Mark paid
```

```sql
-- saving_goals: progress-grid goals; linked to an account or standalone
name          text not null,
target_amount bigint not null,
account_id    uuid references accounts (id), -- linked: progress = account balance
saved_amount  bigint not null default 0,     -- used when unlinked; bumped by Top up
target_on     date,                          -- optional deadline; powers on-track hint
color         text not null
```

```sql
-- budgets: monthly envelope per category (period is implicitly monthly for MVP)
category_id  uuid not null references categories (id) on delete cascade,
amount       bigint not null,
-- unique (space_id, category_id); spent is computed from the month's expense transactions
```

```sql
-- mortgages: shared ownership and standard repayment in one shape
name             text not null,              -- property: "12 Maple Close"
lender           text not null,
original_amount  bigint not null,            -- principal at start; powers paid-off progress
balance          bigint not null,            -- current amount owed (manually updated)
interest_rate    numeric(5,2) not null,      -- percent, e.g. 4.79
rate_type        text not null check (rate_type in ('fixed','variable','tracker')),
rate_ends_on     date,                       -- fix/deal expiry; the remortgage countdown
term_ends_on     date not null,
monthly_payment  bigint not null,
property_value   bigint,                     -- estimate; powers equity/LTV card
equity_share_pct numeric(5,2),               -- shared ownership share; null = 100% owned
rent_monthly     bigint                      -- shared-ownership rent; null when none
```

### 80%-rule notes

- **To `metadata`, not columns:** ground rent, service charge, overpayment allowance (collected since 2026-08-09 as `overpayment_allowance_pct` via the mortgage drawer), mortgage account number, staircasing history, a lone plan name on a subscription (the canonical Netflix "Premium" example), card network artwork variants, paused-subscription state. Promotion rule applies if any goes hot.
- **Sub-80% FK columns kept deliberately:** `transfer_account_id`, `recurring_payment_id`, `card_id`, `saving_goals.account_id` are relational and query-driving (`WHERE`/joins/indexes) — the promotion rule's own criterion. JSONB can't hold enforced FKs.
- `transactions.category_id` clears ~80% across income+expense rows; transfers/adjustments legitimately carry none.
- Watch-items flagged for honest review after real use: `cards.expires_on`, `saving_goals.target_on`, `mortgages.property_value` — kept because they each power a card/alert John described, but they're the first candidates to demote if they stay empty.
- **Balance trigger:** insert/update/delete on `transactions` adjusts `accounts.balance` (and the transfer destination). `Sync balance` writes an `adjustment` transaction for the delta — history always explains the number.

## Flows

- **Add transaction** — drawer, 4 fields (amount, kind, account, description), everything else optional/defaulted. Target: under 10 seconds.
- **Quick transfer** — from any savings/goal context: from-account, to-account, amount. One drawer, no navigation.
- **Sync balance** — popover on an account card: type the real balance → adjustment transaction for the difference.
- **Mark paid** — on any due recurring row: creates the expense transaction (account, amount, category, `recurring_payment_id`) and advances `next_due_on` by cadence. One tap.
- **Add subscription/bill** — drawer: name, kind, amount, cadence, next due (+ optional account/category).
- **Add saving goal / top up** — drawer; top-up is a transfer when linked, a `saved_amount` bump when not.
- **Overpayment what-if** — interactive card; slider drives client-side amortization. Nothing stored.
- **Monthly review** (emergent, not a feature): Overview top-to-bottom — due list clear? budgets green? goals moving? — designed to take under a minute.

## AI-assisted entry

- **Now:** John asks Claude ("add Disney+ at £7.99 monthly"), Claude writes via Supabase directly. The schema is deliberately AI-friendly: flat tables, self-describing names, text+check enums, minimal required fields.
- **Later:** in-app AI entry (chat/quick-add) building on the exact same shapes — a separate epic, not part of E2 MVP.
